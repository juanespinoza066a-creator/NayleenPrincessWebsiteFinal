# Palace 5.1 art sources

Four backgrounds were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Each uses this prompt template:

“Game room background, wide landscape composition. [THEME]. Match a cute polished soft-painted pastel lavender princess game, charming detailed environment. Leave open foreground floor space for a small mascot and four collectible toys, with grounded display surfaces. No characters in foreground, no text, no watermark.”

Themes:
- gambit.webp: a cozy Gambit X-Men fan bedroom, framed posters of Gambit with his brown trench coat and magenta armor, playing-card motifs, glowing pink card lights.
- minecraft.webp: a Minecraft-inspired cozy block-built cottage interior, wooden planks, pixel-like grass and flower pots, crafting table, window onto a blocky garden.
- manor.webp: an Identity V-inspired cozy gothic manor sitting room, antique cipher machine, ornate windows, portrait frames, lavender candles, moonlit garden beyond.
- restaurant.webp: a P.F. Chang’s-inspired elegant Asian restaurant booth, red lanterns, dark wood lattice, tasteful horse sculpture, cozy dining table with dumplings.

Project paths: public/assets/images/rooms/; corresponding native assets: NayleenAndroid/app/src/main/assets/artwork/rooms/.

Hunter portrait icons and full character illustrations are official NetEase Identity V artwork, retrieved from the [official character roster](https://www.identityvgame.com/en/character/index.html) and its character articles, not AI recreations. Copyright remains with the original rightsholders. The icons use the roster portraits; the in-game characters for Antonio/Joseph/Grace use their official illustrations with subtle canvas movement. Ann retains her established animated puppet. The procedural fallback remains while image assets load. Only the current full portrait is loaded to bound memory. Original images remain unaltered; their transparent image bounds are used by Canvas when drawing.
