A little adventure for Nayleen

The gate is locked, princesa.

Ann is prowling the moonlit gardens. Complete three ciphers to unlock the moon gate, then reach it to escape. One treasure is hidden along the paths. Your first escape earns Akali’s outfit reward. This adventure is one run each week; closing the page saves your place.

Explore Move with the thumb stick or WASD. Follow the glowing compass markers.

Decode Tap Interact near a machine. It repairs automatically. Occasionally a calibration ring appears tap when the needle crosses the bright zone. Moving saves your progress.

Psychic stun Tap Psychic stun or press Space when Ann is close (about four garden tiles). She freezes for five seconds. You begin with three charges; completing a cipher restores one, up to three.

Actions Tap Interact, then stay still while the progress bar fills to collect treasure or the peanut, drop a pallet, or open the gate. Moving or taking a hit cancels the action. A missed calibration locks decoding on that cipher for three seconds. Music and effects can be switched off separately.

Escape Break Ann’s line of sight, sprint, drop pallets, or use a psychic stun. Watch your three hearts.
