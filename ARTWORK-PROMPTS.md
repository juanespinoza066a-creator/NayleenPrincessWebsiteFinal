# New outfit artwork — Palace 4.2

Created with the built-in image generator. Each is a single alpha-transparent watercolor/plush portrait of the existing princess Espurr. The final files are under public/assets/images/outfits/ and matching Android artwork/outfits/. References were used for wardrobe details; screenshots/photos are not pasted into the game.

Common prompt: keep the exact lavender Espurr identity, folded ears, purple oval eyes, tiny dot mouth, short round paws/body, small gold princess crown, front-facing whole-body pose, watercolor/plush finish and transparent background.
